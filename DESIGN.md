## Design decisions

- Identity: bone paper + pine + teal signal. Not GoldTrac black-and-gold.
- Signature interaction: Intent → Review → Authorize → Inspect rail. Amount re-check blocks stale approval.
- Demo labeled. No invented volume, customers, or live receipt.
- Menu + full-screen nav, Escape + Close, focus return.
- iPhone: 48px controls, single column under 860px, no horizontal layout grids.
